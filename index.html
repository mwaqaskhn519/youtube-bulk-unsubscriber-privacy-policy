Section 01Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how YouTube Bulk Unsubscriber (“the Extension”) handles your information. In this policy, “we”, “us” and “our” mean the developer of the Extension.

The Extension helps you view, sort and unsubscribe from your own YouTube channel subscriptions, with undo, backups and insights. It works inside your browser, using the YouTube account you are already signed in to. We have no servers, we never receive your YouTube data, and we never sell it.

Section 02Information we access

  • Your subscriptions: the channels you are subscribed to and their public details, such as name, photo, subscriber count, video count, topics and the date of their latest upload. This lets you sort your list and spot inactive channels.
  • Your YouTube account: basic details of the account you are signed in to, such as your name, handle and photo, only to show whose list is open and to keep each account’s settings separate.
  • Your YouTube session: the Extension uses your existing YouTube session in this browser to read your list and to unsubscribe or resubscribe when you ask. It never asks for, reads or stores your password.
  • Your voice (optional): if you turn on voice commands, Chrome’s speech recognition turns what you say into text. If you set up voice lock, the Extension keeps a small numeric voice signature, never a recording.
  • License key: if you buy Pro, the key you enter is used only to check your purchase.

Section 03How we use information

  • To show your subscriptions with search, filters, rules and insights.
  • To unsubscribe only from the channels you choose, always skip your protected channels, and let you undo or resubscribe.
  • To keep your history and the backups you make, and to create the files and reports you export.
  • To refresh your list in the background, and to show the optional weekly digest and right-click actions.
  • To understand voice and AI requests, only when you use those features.
  • To remember your settings, count your free removals and check your license.

Section 04Where your data is kept

Your subscription list, history, backups, rules and settings are saved in your browser, on your own device. None of it is sent to us.

A few small items, such as your voice signature, license, free removal count, protected channels, rules and preferences, are also saved in Chrome’s synced storage and, encrypted, in your browser’s storage for youtube.com. This lets them come back after you reinstall the Extension. They are linked to a one-way code made from your YouTube account, never to your email.

Your controls: Settings → Delete voice removes your voice signature everywhere. Settings → Clear all local data deletes the copy in your browser. Clearing Chrome sync data or site data for youtube.com removes the other copies.

Section 05What we share

We do not sell or share your data. The Extension only connects to these services, and only for the reasons below:

  • YouTube: to read your subscriptions and to unsubscribe or resubscribe, using your own session.
  • Gumroad, our payment provider: when you activate Pro and from time to time after that, your license key and the product ID are sent only to check that your license is valid. No YouTube data is included.
  • Google speech recognition: only while the microphone is on, Chrome sends your voice to Google to turn it into text. The voice lock check runs on your device.
  • Google AI (optional): only if you add your own Google AI key, the text of your request and a short summary of your feed, such as counts and top topics, are sent to Google’s Gemini service. Your account details are never included, and the key stays in this browser. Chrome’s built-in on-device AI sends nothing.

Sharing and bug reports: share buttons open the app you choose with text you can edit first. Bug reports open as an email draft you can read and change; the optional technical details never include channel names, your license key or account details. Nothing is sent unless you send it yourself.

If you buy Pro, the payment happens on Gumroad’s website, under Gumroad’s own terms.

Section 06Our commitments

  • We never sell or rent your data.
  • We never use your data for ads or credit decisions.
  • We use your data only to help you manage your subscriptions.
  • We never add tracking, analytics or outside code.

Section 07Children

The Extension is not intended for children.
